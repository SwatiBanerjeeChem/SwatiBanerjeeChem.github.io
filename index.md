## About me

I am a research scientist developing high-resolution mass spectrometry workflows to understand protein structure, interactions, and function.

Across 10 years in research institutes, pharmaceutical quality control, and contract research, I have built expertise spanning cross-linking mass spectrometry, high-resolution LC–MS/MS, analytical method development, and complex data interpretation. My work turns experimental measurements into residue-level views of protein interactions and conformational change.

## Research interests

My doctoral research at IOCB Prague develops and applies cross-linking mass spectrometry workflows to reveal protein-interaction interfaces, spatial constraints, and conformational changes in complex biological systems.

### Cross-linking mass spectrometry

- Cleavable and non-cleavable cross-linkers
- Sample preparation and LC–MS/MS acquisition
- Cross-linked peptide identification and validation
- Protein-interaction and residue-level distance mapping

### High-resolution LC–MS/MS

- Orbitrap Fusion Lumos mass spectrometer
- Dionex Ultimate 3000 RSLC nano
- Bottom-up proteomics
- Protein and small-molecule characterization

### Structural interpretation

- Interaction-interface mapping
- Investigation of conformational changes
- MeroX, pLink, xiVIEW, PyMOL, and Chimera

### Pharmaceutical analysis

- HPLC/UHPLC assays and impurity profiling
- Compendial testing according to USP, EP, and IP standards
- Method development in an ISO 17025 environment
