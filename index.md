<section class="dark-section" id="research">
  <div class="section-grid">
    <div class="section-label">02 / Research</div>
    <div class="section-content">
      <p class="eyebrow">Current focus</p>
      <h2>Seeing proteins through the links between them.</h2>
      <p class="muted">My doctoral research at IOCB Prague develops and applies cross-linking mass spectrometry workflows to reveal protein interaction interfaces, spatial constraints, and conformational changes in complex biological systems.</p>
      <div class="expertise-grid">
        <article><span>01</span><h3>Cross-linking MS</h3><p>Cleavable and non-cleavable cross-linkers, sample preparation, acquisition, and confident identification.</p></article>
        <article><span>02</span><h3>High-resolution LC–MS/MS</h3><p>Orbitrap Fusion Lumos and Dionex Ultimate 3000 RSLC nano workflows for protein characterization.</p></article>
        <article><span>03</span><h3>Structural interpretation</h3><p>Residue-level interaction mapping and visualization with MeroX, pLink, xiVIEW, PyMOL, and Chimera.</p></article>
        <article><span>04</span><h3>Pharmaceutical analysis</h3><p>HPLC/UHPLC assays, impurity profiling, compendial testing, and method development in an ISO 17025 environment.</p></article>
