## About {#about}

I am a research scientist with 10 years of combined experience in pharmaceutical quality control and mass-spectrometry-based structural proteomics across a leading research institute, a contract research organization, and a government regulatory laboratory.

My work focuses on developing high-resolution LC–MS/MS workflows for protein-interaction studies in complex biological systems. I use cross-linking mass spectrometry to map residue-level interaction interfaces and conformational changes, connecting analytical precision with biological questions.

## Research interests {#research}

- **Cross-linking mass spectrometry** — cleavable and non-cleavable cross-linkers, sample preparation, acquisition, and confident identification.
- **Structural proteomics** — residue-level interaction mapping and investigation of protein conformational changes.
- **High-resolution LC–MS/MS** — Orbitrap Fusion Lumos and nano-LC workflows for protein characterization.
- **Analytical method development** — HPLC/UHPLC assays, impurity profiling, and complex pharmaceutical analysis.
- **Protein analysis** — bottom-up proteomics, SDS-PAGE, and in-gel and in-solution protein digestion.
